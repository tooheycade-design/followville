# Followville Arcade

## Canonical placement

- City day: 44
- Population: 1,784 (unchanged)
- Building count: 1,781 (unchanged; this is a redevelopment)
- Canonical seed: 129
- Grid address: `(4, -3)`
- World centre: approximately `(64.5, -38.5)`
- State type/name: `arcade` / `Followville Arcade`

Seed 129 was selected only after checking the built downtown, the canonical
state, and the live claims data. It was an unclaimed original-downtown house
parcel with strong street, elevated, overhead, entrance, and three-quarter
visibility. The adjacent seed 130 property is claimed and was not changed.
The existing seed 524 Followville Cinema also remains untouched.

The database sync contains an intentionally narrow conversion for seed 129. It
will convert only an unclaimed, claimable `house` row into a non-claimable
`arcade` row. A claim or any unexpected metadata makes the sync fail rather
than overwrite citizen ownership.

## Exterior design

The arcade is a three-level stepped urban jewel-box rather than a decorated
box. Its navy, brick, brass, cyan, and magenta palette follows the established
low-poly city materials. The facade includes a deep illuminated marquee,
exact `FOLLOWVILLE ARCADE` lettering, a blade sign, tall storefront glazing,
an articulated entrance, vertical neon fins, upper windows, a stepped crown,
roof screening and HVAC, planters, a bench, bike hoops, and integrated
sidewalk lighting. Four practical lights support the night exterior while the
emissive details remain readable in the web export.

## Interior design

The interior keeps a clear central camera aisle from the doors to the rear
redemption counter. It includes ceiling baffles and light bars, floor guide
strips, wall graphics, a change kiosk, ticket checker, and five deliberately
different low-poly machine families:

1. Upright cabinet -- shaped cabinet, marquee, inset screen, controls, and base
   lighting.
2. Claw machine -- glazed prize bay, toy prizes, gantry, suspended cable and
   claw, control panel, and retrieval hatch.
3. Pinball machine -- four legs, sloped playfield, bumpers, rails, controls,
   and illuminated score backbox.
4. Racing machine -- bucket seat, pedals, steering column and wheel, console,
   screen, and overhead header.
5. Rhythm machine -- large screen and marquee, speakers, hand rails, and four
   individually lit floor pads.

The previous flat back-wall display was replaced with a custom `PRIZE VAULT`
redemption table. Its modeled prizes are a faceted ball, trophy, rocket, toy
car, and small robot/plush-style figure. Machines are instanced as repeated
families and merged into the parcel asset for efficient city rendering.

## Performance and review

The final isolated asset report records 2,277 vertices, 1,683 polygons, 20
materials, and four lights. The building stays within its one-lot footprint
and contains no human NPCs.

Review renders are generated by `scripts/render_arcade_review.py` under
`renders/arcade_review/`. The set covers day front, both day obliques,
overhead, night front and oblique, entrance push-in, central interior aisle,
and both machine rows. `asset_report.json` in the same folder records the
measured asset statistics.
